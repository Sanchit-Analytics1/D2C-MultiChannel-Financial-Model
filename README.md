# Multi-Channel D2C Financial Model & Executive Dashboard

A dynamic, multi-tier financial model built in Excel for analyzing multi-channel D2C revenue performance, automated P&L budget-variance tracking, cash settlement reconciliation, and executive KPI reporting (June 2026 – October 2026).

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project models financial performance across three primary distribution channels: **Shopify**, **Marketplace**, and **Retail Store**. It converts raw transactional data into dynamic P&L insights, rigorous budget-variance evaluations, and real-time cash flow reconciliation.

---

## 🏗️ Model Architecture (5-Tab Workbook)

* **`Sales_Data`** : Transactional data engine tracking 100+ multi-month revenue streams, discounts, COGS, and channel attribution.
* **`Channel_Summary`** : Dynamic Pivot aggregation layer summarizing Gross Revenue, Net Sales, and Contribution Margin.
* **`Budget_Variance`** : Standardized variance engine (`Actual - Budget`) with automated Favorable/Unfavorable impact tagging across channels.
* **`Management_Dashboard`** : Executive-ready visual dashboard featuring real-time KPI cards, budget attainment metrics, and 3 distinct performance charts.
* **`Settlement_Reconciliation`** : Cash flow engine adjusting net sales for payment gateway fees (2%), platform commissions & logistics (15%), and Cash-on-Delivery (COD) rolling reserves to determine exact net bank payouts.

---

## 📊 Key Financial Insights & Metrics
* **Total Net Sales**: ₹10,23,500 across a multi-month reporting window (June–October 2026) achieving **108.3% budget attainment**.
* **Contribution Margin**: Delivered ₹3,37,896 in total contribution margin (~33.0% margin efficiency).
* **Cost Variance Analysis**: Identified baseline budget misalignments in Marketplace logistics vs. actual unit economic ratios.
* **Cash Flow Realism**: Reconciled gross receivables down to exact net bank settlements of **₹9,19,532** after accounting for gateway and platform deductions.
